Make it yours

Start in the free community demo; no local model installation is needed for this route. For ComfyUI, use the full setup guide (.md) instead.

  1. Upload your original reference to the demo. Use a synthetic character or a consenting adult. Do not send private client images to a public third-party demo.
  2. Choose a shot above and copy its instruction. Replace the example's appearance sentence with the traits visible in your own reference, including hair and clothing.
  3. Use that card's recorded seed and likeness value. Set grounding to 768, steps to 10, guidance to 0.0, and random seed off. The demo's LoRA is fused at 1.0.
  4. Run one shot and review face, pose, hands, clothing and light before keeping it. Reuse the original reference for every shot, never a generated result. Stop if the free quota is exhausted; no paid plan is needed for this pilot.

One person, one wardrobe, still images only. The full-body shot invents trousers, shoes and body details absent from the head-and-shoulders reference. No identity guarantee, automatic face matching or video consistency claim.
